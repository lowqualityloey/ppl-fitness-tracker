🌟 Confidence Boosters

  • Leg machines are usually grouped together - easy to find
  • Start light on squats - everyone builds up gradually
  • Leg press is perfect for beginners - very safe machine
  • Don't worry about soreness tomorrow - it's normal for leg day
  • Most people take longer rests on leg day - it's okay
  • Ask gym staff to show you machine adjustments
  • Leg workouts are tough for everyone - you're not alone
  • Focus on form over weight - legs respond well to proper movement
  • It's normal to feel shaky after leg day
  • Everyone starts somewhere with leg strength

🤝 Gym Etiquette

  • Wipe down machine seats after use
  • Adjust machine settings back to neutral position
  • Return dumbbells to correct spots on rack
  • Don't drop heavy weights - control the descent
  • Let others know if you're doing multiple sets on machines
  • Keep walkways clear of equipment

🏋️ Equipment Guide

  • Leg machine area: Leg press, leg curls, leg extensions
  • Dumbbell area: For goblet squats and Romanian deadlifts
  • Calf machine area: Standing or seated calf raise machines
  • Cardio area: Treadmills, bikes, ellipticals
  • Stretching area: Mats, usually near group fitness rooms
