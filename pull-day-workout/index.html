Pull Day Confidence Tips

Equipment
  • Cable machines are perfect for back training - very beginner friendly
  • Most cable machines have pictures showing proper form
  • Ask gym staff to show you how to adjust cable height
Exercise
  • Everyone starts light on back exercises - form is more important
  • Focus on squeezing your shoulder blades together
  • Pulling exercises help improve posture from desk work
Social
  • Most people are focused on their own workout
  • You can ask to 'work in' during rest periods
  • Everyone was a beginner once - don't feel self-conscious
