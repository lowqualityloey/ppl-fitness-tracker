  • Wipe down equipment after each use
  • Re-rack weights in correct locations

  • Maintain appropriate personal space
  • Ask for help when needed - most people are happy to assist

🏋️ Equipment Guide

  • Free weights area: Dumbbells, barbells, benches
  • Cable machines: Pulleys with various attachments
  • Machine area: Chest press, leg press, etc.
  • Cardio area: Treadmills, bikes, ellipticals
  • Stretching area: Mats, usually near group fitness rooms
