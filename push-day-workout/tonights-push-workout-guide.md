# 🏋️‍♂️ TONIGHT'S PUSH DAY WORKOUT
**Monday, September 8, 2025 | Total Time: 45-55 minutes**

---

## 🔥 PHASE 1: WARM-UP (10 minutes)
**Location: Start at cardio area, then open space**

1. **5 min Light Cardio** - Treadmill or bike, start slow
2. **Arm Circles** - 10 each direction (small → big)
3. **Shoulder Rolls** - 10 forward, 10 backward
4. **Push-up to Downward Dog** - 5 reps, open up chest

---

## 💪 PHASE 2: MAIN WORKOUT (25-30 minutes)
**Remember: Start light, focus on form!**

### Exercise 1: Bench Press (Dumbbell or Barbell)
- **Sets:** 3 x 8-10 reps
- **Rest:** 60-90 seconds
- **Location:** Free weights/bench area
- **Weight:** Start with 5-10kg dumbbells OR just the barbell (20kg)

### Exercise 2: Dumbbell Overhead Press
- **Sets:** 3 x 8-10 reps  
- **Rest:** 60-90 seconds
- **Location:** Dumbbell area
- **Weight:** 2.5-7.5kg dumbbells
- **Tip:** Keep core tight, press straight up

### Exercise 3: Chest Press Machine
- **Sets:** 3 x 10-12 reps
- **Rest:** 60-90 seconds
- **Location:** Machine area
- **Weight:** Start with lightest setting
- **Tip:** Control both pressing and lowering

### Exercise 4: Lateral Raises
- **Sets:** 3 x 12-15 reps
- **Rest:** 60-90 seconds
- **Location:** Dumbbell area
- **Weight:** 1-3kg dumbbells (very light!)
- **Tip:** Lift to shoulder height, control down

### Exercise 5: Cable Tricep Pushdowns
- **Sets:** 3 x 10-12 reps
- **Rest:** 60-90 seconds
- **Location:** Cable machine area
- **Weight:** Start light
- **Tip:** Keep elbows at your sides, only forearms move

### Exercise 6: Push-ups (Bodyweight)
- **Sets:** 2 x 8-12 reps
- **Rest:** 60-90 seconds
- **Location:** Open floor space
- **Modification:** Do on knees if regular push-ups are too hard

---

## 🧘‍♂️ PHASE 3: STRETCHING (10 minutes)
**Location: Stretching area or mat area**

1. **Chest Doorway Stretch** - 30 seconds
   - Stand in doorway, lean forward gently
   
2. **Overhead Tricep Stretch** - 30 seconds each arm
   - Reach one arm overhead, pull elbow with other hand
   
3. **Cross-body Shoulder Stretch** - 30 seconds each arm
   - Pull arm across chest, hold at elbow
   
4. **Child's Pose** - 45 seconds
   - Kneel, sit back on heels, reach arms forward
   
5. **Cat-Cow Stretch** - 30 seconds
   - On hands and knees, alternate arching and rounding back

---

## 🚴‍♂️ PHASE 4: POST-WORKOUT CARDIO (10 minutes)
**Location: Back to cardio area**

- **Easy Walk or Bike** - 10 minutes at comfortable pace
- **Goal:** Cool down, not to get more tired
- **Heart rate:** Should be able to hold conversation
